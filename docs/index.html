Open geodata catalogue

Spatial outputs of 7 open studies by David Oloyede, as a static STAC catalogue and as an OGC API Features service you can run yourself.

David OloyedeBuilt 2026-11-19

Overview

Each study publishes its maps and tables on its own page. This catalogue lists the spatial layers behind them in one place, in open formats with checksums, so they can be found, downloaded and loaded into GIS software or code without reading each repository. The layers are the studies' own outputs and keep their limits; the study pages explain what each one can and cannot show.

Collections

One collection per study. The small maps show each collection's extent (dashed) and its items, on country outlines.

  • Floods, droughts and design rainfall in two data-sparse Nigerian basins

    Study areas and per-cell design rainfall from a study of floods, drought and extreme rainfall in the Lower Niger-Benue reach and the Sokoto-Rima basin, Nigeria, using satellite and reanalysis data where gauges are missing.

    2 items: 2 vector1981–2070Data CC BY 4.0

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Ogun River catchment-to-coast environmental observatory

    Sub-basin units of the Ogun River catchment and Lagos Lagoon with summary indicators (rainfall, NDVI, turbidity index, Sentinel-1 inundation, soil moisture) from Earth observation, 2019–2024.

    1 item: 1 vector2019–2024Data CC BY 4.0

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Testing a satellite water balance for groundwater recharge: Upper Ouémé and Upper Ogun

    Basin outlines and gridded water-balance maps (CHIRPS rainfall, WaPOR evapotranspiration and their difference) for the Upper Ouémé (Benin) and Upper Ogun (Nigeria), 2018–2024. The surplus is rainfall minus evapotranspiration from satellite products; it is not measured recharge.

    21 items: 1 vector, 20 raster2018–2024Data CC BY 4.0

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Water storage in the Lake Chad basin from GRACE and GRACE-FO

    Study areas and the per-cell trend in total water storage from the GRACE and GRACE-FO gravity missions over the Lake Chad basin. The gravity record measures all stored water; groundwater is not separated from surface water, and there is no well-based validation.

    2 items: 2 vector2002–2026Data CC BY 4.0

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Surface urban heat island and distance to vegetation in Lagos State

    Local government area (LGA) boundaries of Lagos State with land surface temperature, vegetation, population and hot-spot attributes from the study's earlier Earth Engine export. The current paper's heat-gap results are computed on 60 m rasters that are not stored in the repository.

    1 item: 1 vector2022–2024Data CC BY 4.0

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Urban sealing and the shallow water table of the Lekki–Epe barrier, Lagos

    Recharge-potential index maps of the Lekki–Epe barrier for a 1986–1996 baseline (Landsat 5) and for 2023–2024 (Sentinel-2), from the study's re-analysis. The index is a relative score, not recharge, and the study reports that the size of its decline is not robust.

    2 items: 2 raster1986–2024Data not stated separately

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

  • Travel time to health facilities in Mozambique

    District outlines of Mozambique with modelled travel time to the nearest public-sector health facility, and the facility lists used. Travel times are model estimates from friction surfaces and modelled population, not observed journeys; the study shows how much they change with the facility list, mode of travel and population layer.

    3 items: 3 vector2019–2024Data various, see note

    Study pageRepositorySTAC collection

    Use the data

    The catalogue root is stac/catalog.json. All links inside it are relative, so it can be copied or mirrored as a folder. Vector layers come as GeoParquet and GeoJSON in longitude–latitude (EPSG:4326); rasters as Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs in their original grid, with a PNG quick-look. Each file has its size and SHA-256 checksum in the STAC item (file:size, file:checksum).

    import pystac, geopandas as gpd
cat = pystac.Catalog.from_file("https://oloyededavid.github.io/Open-Geodata-Catalogue/stac/catalog.json")
item = cat.get_child("observational-hydrology-extremes").get_item("study-areas")
gdf = gpd.read_parquet(item.assets["geoparquet"].get_absolute_href())

    A Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFF can be read over HTTP without downloading it first, for example in QGIS (Add Raster Layer, protocol HTTP) or with GDAL: gdalinfo /vsicurl/https://oloyededavid.github.io/Open-Geodata-Catalogue/stac/…/item.tif.

    OGC API Features

    The 10 vector layers can also be served as OGC API Features collections from PostGIS with pygeoapi, so that GIS clients can query them by bounding box and page through features. This website is static and cannot run that service. To run it, clone the repository and start it with Docker Compose, or locally with PostgreSQL, PostGIS and pygeoapi installed (see the README):

    docker compose up
curl "http://localhost:5000/collections"
curl "http://localhost:5000/collections/observational_hydrology_extremes_study_areas/items?limit=5&bbox=3,5,10,17"

    At the last local check (2026-11-19), the service served 10 collections with 4,162 features from PostGIS 3.4.2 with pygeoapi 0.24.0, and every check passed: paging with limit, single features, bounding-box filtering and the core, GeoJSON and OpenAPI conformance classes. pygeoapi declares 18 conformance classes in all; the others were not tested here, and no formal OGC test suite was run.

    How the catalogue and the service are built, and what was and was not checked, is described on the design page.

    Limits

    • The layers are copies of the studies' outputs at the commits recorded in each collection. If a study changes, the catalogue is out of date until it is rebuilt.
    • Most layers are satellite or model estimates (rainfall, evapotranspiration, gravity, land surface temperature), not ground measurements. Their uncertainty and validation are described in the studies, not here.
    • Only outputs already stored in the study repositories are included. Large intermediate rasters that the studies rebuild from public archives are not.
