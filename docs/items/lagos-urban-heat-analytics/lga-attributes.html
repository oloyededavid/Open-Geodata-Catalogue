LGA boundaries with heat and vegetation attributes

The 20 LGAs of Lagos State with area, modelled population, mean Landsat and MODIS land surface temperature, NDVI, NDBI, terrain and Getis-Ord hot-spot statistics, from the export recorded in the layer's own `period` field. Population is a WorldPop model estimate, not a census count.
