Total water storage trend per 1-degree cell

Straight-line trend of total water storage (mm per year) in each 1-degree GRACE/GRACE-FO cell (COST-G solution). A trend over the whole record; the study reports that the record changes little until about 2018 and then rises, so one straight line is a simplification.
