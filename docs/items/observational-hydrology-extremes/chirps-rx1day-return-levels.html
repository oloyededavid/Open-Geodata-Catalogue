CHIRPS design rainfall per 0.25-degree cell, 1985–2014

Return levels of annual maximum daily rainfall (Rx1day, mm) for 2, 10, 25, 50 and 100 years, from a GEV fit by L-moments to CHIRPS 1985–2014, for each 0.25-degree cell of the two study areas. Satellite rainfall estimates, not gauge measurements.
