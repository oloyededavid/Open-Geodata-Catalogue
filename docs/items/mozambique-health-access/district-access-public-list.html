District travel time to public-sector facilities, 2020

The 159 districts with WorldPop 2020 population and, for the public-sector facility list of Maina et al. (2019) (list A), the share of residents over 60 and 120 minutes from a facility and the median travel time in minutes, by motorized and walking travel (Malaria Atlas Project friction surfaces, 2020). `compared` marks the districts used in the study's comparisons. Outlines simplified by the study.
